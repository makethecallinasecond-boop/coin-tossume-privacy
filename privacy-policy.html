Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Coin Tossume does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Local Data

The app stores some settings locally on your device, including:

  • Total flip count
  • Pending flip count
  • Sound setting
  • Total flip display setting

This data is stored only on your device and is used to provide app functionality.

Firestore Usage

Coin Tossume uses Firebase Firestore to maintain a global flip counter. Only aggregated flip count increments are sent to Firebase. This information is not linked to your identity.

Analytics and Tracking

Coin Tossume does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

Sharing

The app allows users to share result images and text using the iOS share sheet.

Third-Party Services

Firebase Firestore is provided by Google Firebase. Their privacy practices may apply.
